Description

Excellent detached two storey property built in 2005 and standing on a large corner site. The property has oil fired heating, double glazed windows and Palladio front door and is in pristine condition throughout.

There is a large cobble lock driveway to the front and side access to rear enclosed garden with large, detached shed.

Large green are to the front of the property It is within walking distance of all town centre amenities and just 5 minutes to junction 10 & 11 of the M8 motorway and 10 minutes to Clonmel.
